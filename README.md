# MY-TV-Telugu

A Telugu-language-only M3U playlist for VLC and compatible IPTV players.

## Main playlist

`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Nani9133/MY-TV-Telugu/main/telugu.m3u`

Paste this URL into VLC with **Media → Open Network Stream** (Ctrl+N).

## Channel groups

- Telugu Entertainment
- Telugu Movies
- Telugu Music
- Telugu News
- Telugu Devotional
- Telugu Kids
- Telugu General

## Quality notes

- The playlist prefers the highest publicly listed source quality for each channel.
- 1080p is Full HD; 720p is HD; 576p/480p are SD.
- No verified genuine 2160p/4K Telugu channel source was found in the public channel directory used to assemble this playlist. A player cannot turn an SD/HD source into genuine 4K.
- Quality labels are directory-reported and can change at the broadcaster/CDN. Actual playback and resolution may vary by region, device, and stream availability.

## Availability and rights

This playlist contains publicly listed stream URLs; it does not host or re-broadcast video. Public stream URLs can stop working or become geo-restricted. Premium channels such as Star Maa, Zee Telugu, Gemini TV and paid sports channels are not included unless a legitimate publicly available stream is identified. Use streams only where you are authorized to access them.
