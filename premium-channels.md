## Official viewing options

- **ETV:** The official ETV Telugu website lists its channels and a Live TV option: https://www.etv.co.in/channels/
- **ETV via Tata Play Binge:** Tata Play Binge's ETV Win offering includes seven live ETV channels for subscribers. Availability is subject to the current service package: https://www.etvwin.com/
- **Zee Telugu and Zee Cinemalu:** ZEE5 lists Telugu live channels including Zee Telugu HD and Zee Cinemalu HD. Live TV access depends on the ZEE5 plan and account: https://www.zee5.com/live-tv/lang/telugu-channels
- **Star Maa and Star Maa Movies:** Check the official JioStar/JioHotstar or your DTH/cable provider's subscribed lineup. Tata Play lists Star Maa and Star Maa Movies in its Telugu channel packs: https://www.tataplay.com/dth/pack/telugu-regional
- **Star Maa Music:** Listed by Tata Play in its Telugu regional lineup. Check your provider's current subscription and app support: https://www.tataplay.com/dth/pack/telugu-regional
- **Sun Gemini TV, Gemini Movies and Gemini Music:** Sun Group confirms these Telugu channels; Tata Play lists the channels in its Telugu packs: https://www.sun.in/television.aspx and https://www.tataplay.com/dth/pack/telugu-regional

## Why these are not inserted as fake M3U URLs

A channel appearing in an official app or DTH package does not mean its licensed live feed is a public, direct HLS URL. Premium services commonly use account authentication, expiring playback tokens and/or DRM. A web page or app URL is not a playable M3U stream.

This repository will only add these channels to `telugu.m3u` when a broadcaster or authorized distributor publishes a direct stream that is permitted for this use. It will not copy private, tokenized, scraped or unauthorized feeds from third-party IPTV repositories.
